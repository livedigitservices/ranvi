# RANVI INSTITUTIONS — ADMINISTRATOR & FACULTY USER GUIDE

Welcome to the Ranvi Institutions administrative portal guide.

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Portal

- Login URL: `/login`
- Default Credentials:
  - **Super Admin**: `admin@ranviinstitutions.com` / `RanviAdmin@2026!`
  - **Faculty**: `faculty.rajesh@ranviinstitutions.com` / `RanviFaculty@2026!`

---

## 2. Live Zoom Class Management

1. Navigate to **Live Classes (Zoom)** in the admin sidebar.
2. Click **Schedule New Class**.
3. Provide:
   - Class Title & Subject.
   - Associated Course (e.g. JAIIB Master Certification 2026).
   - Faculty Lead.
   - Date, Start Time (24hr), and Duration in Minutes.
   - **Zoom Meeting URL** (must be a valid `zoom.us` link).
   - Optional Zoom Meeting ID & Passcode.
4. Click **Schedule Class**. The platform immediately schedules the session and broadcasts an automated notification to all students enrolled in that course.
5. In case of unexpected changes, clicking **Cancel Class** sends an instant alert to affected students.

---

## 3. Single-Device Session Enforcement & Resetting Student Devices

To prevent account sharing while keeping the system fair for legitimate students:
1. When a student logs in, their current browser is registered as their active session.
2. If the student purchases a new phone or changes computers, signing in on the new device transfers their session.
3. If a student is locked or encounters browser cookie errors, an administrator can open **Students & Devices** in the admin sidebar.
4. Locate the student, click **Reset Device**, enter an audit reason (e.g., "Student verified phone replacement"), and click **Confirm Reset**.
5. All reset actions are logged to the immutable **Security Audit Logs**.

---

## 4. Mock Tests & Question Bank Management

1. Navigate to **Mock Tests & Papers**.
2. Click **Create Model Paper** to define duration, total marks, pass marks, negative marking, and free demo status.
3. Click **Manage MCQs** on any paper to add questions with 4 options, designate the correct answer key, and provide step-by-step solutions for student review.

---

## 5. Offline Desk Admissions & Manual Course Grants

1. If a student pays offline at the institute counter, navigate to **Payments & Revenue**.
2. Click **Grant Manual Access**.
3. Select the registered student, choose the course or test series package, specify validity in days (e.g., 180), and provide the offline counter receipt number as the audit reason.
4. Click **Grant Enrolment**. The student immediately receives full learning portal access.
